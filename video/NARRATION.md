# Narration (English, ~140 words, one line per scene)

The on-screen captions stay as short key phrases; the voice says the full line.
Each scene's length follows its line once the voice is generated.

| Scene | Line |
|---|---|
| hook | On a construction site, an inspector takes one photo and writes one line. |
| upload | In Baseer, they drop in the photos, add the note, and name the site. |
| report | About thirty seconds later: a full report. Each finding with its severity, its evidence frame, and the fix. |
| chat | Ask Baseer about it. Answers come only from this evidence, even a draft notice for the contractor. |
| case | Every finding opens a case. Assign it, verify the fix with an after-photo, and only a person can approve the closure. |
| live | Or point a live camera at the site. Each violation raises an alert the moment it appears. |
| records | Deadlines follow severity, overdue cases rise to the top, and every action is signed into the audit log. |
| arabic | Arabic first, right to left, with a dark theme for the control room. |
| end | Baseer. From walkthrough to verified closure. |

Voice used: local Kokoro `af_heart` (ElevenLabs key was invalid). Music: a calm, confident product bed (~100 bpm), entering on a strong bar,
ducked under the voice (~0.14), 1.5 s fade-out under the end card.
