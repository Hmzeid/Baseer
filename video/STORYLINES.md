# Storylines

**Picked: A** — a stranger follows one real photo from upload to a closed case,
which is exactly how Baseer works; B and C stay here so a later pass can switch.

## A. One photo, start to finish (recommended, built)
Device: the worked example. Form: one product world, the camera travelling
through the real UI. A real scaffold photo becomes the upload thumbnail, the
report, the chat answer and a case a person closes; then the film widens to the
live camera, the dashboards and Arabic/dark.
- First 2 s: the scaffold photo full-bleed, "One site photo. One inspector's note."
- Beats: hook → upload → report → chat → case loop → live camera → oversight → Arabic/dark → end card
- Look: the app's own light ground #F3F5F8, navy #0A2540 caption plates, gold #C9A94E accent word; real 2× screenshots.
- Sound: silent delivery (no music source signed in); music bed + click/pop on cursor actions when added.

## B. Monday on site
Device: a moment in someone's day. A site manager opens the dashboard, sees three
overdue cases, walks the scaffold with the live camera, approves a fix.
- First 2 s: the Overview with "3 overdue" landing in red.
- Look: same product world, dark theme for the control room.

## C. The loop that never closes itself
Device: one diagram that grows. Observe → Understand → Explain → Act → Verify → Close,
each node zooming into the real screen that does it, ending on the human approval.
- First 2 s: the six-step loop drawn as a ring, "Close" greyed out.
- Look: navy brand world with gold ring, screens as stops on the ring.
