Design principles behind this look

  • Keynote-light: warm white surfaces, one ink color, navy and gold used sparingly — nothing glows, nothing gradients for decoration.
  • Type does the work: large, confident numerals and headings; small quiet labels. The hierarchy is the design.
  • Severity is a system, not a sticker: tinted chips in lists, solid chips on findings, a colored rail on every card — readable from across a room.
  • Arabic and English are equals: full RTL mirroring, Arabic type set with the same care as Latin, one toggle everywhere.
  • Evidence is visual: the segmentation scene and the evidence thumbnails carry the product's story before a word is read.
